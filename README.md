# Zydus Wellness Product Sales Performance | Descriptive Analytics (2019–2025)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Zydus Wellness product sales performance from 2019 to 2025 using descriptive analytics. It explores product-wise sales, regional demand, customer ratings, revenue, profitability, pricing differences, and yearly sales trends to identify important business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

**Note:** The report uses a synthetic dataset designed to represent realistic FMCG health and wellness industry patterns because Zydus Wellness's actual transaction-level data is not publicly accessible.

## Objectives
- Identify the best-selling products by sales volume.
- Analyze yearly sales trends from 2019 to 2025.
- Compare product demand across North, South, East, and West India.
- Evaluate customer ratings and satisfaction levels.
- Analyze revenue and profit performance by product and region.
- Compare retail prices and ex-factory costs across regions.
- Identify the highest-revenue product-region combinations.
- Recommend improvements based on analytical findings.

## Key Findings

### 1. Best-Selling Products
Glucon-D Regular leads in sales volume with approximately 55,753 units sold, followed by Complan Classic with 49,793 units. ZeroSugar Cola Mix records the lowest sales volume, indicating an opportunity to investigate its market demand and product positioning.

### 2. Yearly Sales Trends
Sales declined in 2020 but recovered in 2021 and continued increasing through 2025. Annual sales grew from approximately 26,545 units in 2019 to 74,065 units in 2025, representing overall growth of around 179%.

### 3. Regional Demand
North India leads in total sales volume, followed by West India, South India, and East India. This regional ranking remains consistent throughout the analyzed period.

### 4. Revenue Analysis
Complan Classic is the highest-revenue product, generating approximately ₹1.98 crore. The Complan Classic–North India combination is the largest individual product-region revenue contributor, generating approximately ₹66.6 lakh.

### 5. Profitability Analysis
West India records the highest average profit per unit at approximately ₹50.4, while East India records the lowest at approximately ₹39.2. This highlights differences in regional pricing, logistics, costs, and product mix.

### 6. Customer Ratings
The average product rating remains stable at approximately 4.10 out of 5. Sugar Free Natura receives one of the highest average ratings, while ZeroSugar Cola Mix is among the lowest-rated products.

### 7. Regional Pricing
East India has the highest average retail price, at approximately ₹270.5, and the largest gap between retail price and ex-factory cost. West India has the lowest average retail price and the smallest gap.

## Business Recommendations
- Improve profitability in North India by reviewing discounts, pricing, and product mix.
- Maintain strong inventory and distribution for Complan Classic, particularly in North India.
- Investigate the lower sales and ratings of ZeroSugar Cola Mix.
- Study the pricing and profitability practices of West India and South India.
- Monitor customer ratings regularly to identify changes in satisfaction.
- Use historical sales data to develop future sales and revenue forecasts.
